Privacy Policy

App: 2048 Classic (com.mironshoh.puzzle2048)
Developer: Mironshoh
Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app 2048 Classic ("the app") handles information. In short: the app does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and does not access your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

2. Data stored on your device

To let you continue a game and keep your best score, the app saves the following on your device only: your current board, your current score, your best score, and your sound and vibration settings. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

3. Internet access, advertising and analytics

The app works fully offline and does not request internet permission. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools.

4. Permissions

The app uses only the vibration permission, to give haptic feedback when tiles merge. You can switch this off inside the app.

5. Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
