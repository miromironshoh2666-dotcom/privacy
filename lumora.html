Privacy Policy

App: Lumora IPTV Player (com.mironshoh.lumoraiptv)
Developer: Mironshoh
Last updated: October 31, 2026

Lumora IPTV Player ("the app") is a media player. It does not provide, host or sell any TV channels, playlists or video content. You add your own sources. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: the developer does not collect, receive, share or sell any personal data.

1. Information we collect

We collect nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking tools. We never receive your playlists, usernames, passwords or viewing history.

2. Data stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following on your device only: the playlists you add (name, link or server address, and the username and password if you add an Xtream account), the channel list and programme guide downloaded from those sources, your favorites, your recently watched list, your locked channel groups and a hashed parental-control PIN. This data never leaves your device through the developer, and it is deleted when you remove a playlist, clear the history, or uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Network access

The app uses the internet only to connect directly from your device to the playlist, programme guide and video stream addresses that you enter. Those servers are operated by third parties chosen by you and may have their own privacy practices. The developer does not operate or see this traffic. Some streams use unencrypted http connections, which is why the app allows them.

4. Purchases

Premium is offered as a subscription with a free trial and as a one-time lifetime purchase, processed entirely by Google Play. The developer does not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether you currently have an active purchase so it can unlock playback.

5. Permissions

The app uses the internet permission to load your playlists, play streams and check purchases with Google Play. If you choose a playlist file, the app reads only that file through Android's file picker. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

6. Children's privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. Parental control lets you lock channel groups with a PIN.

7. Content responsibility

You are responsible for making sure you have the right to use the playlists and streams you add.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
