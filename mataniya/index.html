Privacy Policy

App: MATANIYA – Дурак 3D (com.mataniya.durak) | Developer: M-Tech Developer | Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android card game MATANIYA ("the app") handles information. The app does not require an account, email or phone number.

1. Data stored on your device

Your nickname, language, points, game speed, statistics and saved games are stored locally on your device only. This data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Online games (Google Firebase)

When you choose to play online, the app signs you in anonymously with Firebase Authentication, which creates a random user ID. Your nickname and the state of the game rooms you create or join are stored in Cloud Firestore so that the other players in the room can see them. Rooms are deleted when all players leave, and unused anonymous accounts are deleted automatically after 30 days. Firebase may process technical data such as IP address and device information; see Firebase privacy information.

3. Advertising

The app offers optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob (watch an ad to get bonus points). To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and device and app information. We do not receive this data. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

5. How to delete your data (MATANIYA – Дурак 3D, M-Tech Developer)

  1. Data on your phone (nickname, points, settings, saved games): open Android Settings → Apps → MATANIYA → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. It is deleted immediately.
  2. Online data (anonymous player ID, nickname inside game rooms): send an e-mail to miromironshoh2666@gmail.com with the subject "MATANIYA data deletion" and your in-game nickname.
  3. We delete the anonymous account and any rooms linked to it within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted: anonymous Firebase user ID, nickname and game room data. Nothing is kept after deletion. Game rooms are also deleted automatically when all players leave, and unused anonymous accounts are deleted automatically after 30 days. Advertising data is processed by Google; you can reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

7. Contact
