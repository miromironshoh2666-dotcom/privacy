Privacy Policy

App: Tile Slide (com.mironshoh.tileslide) | Developer: M-Tech Developer | Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Tile Slide ("the app") handles information. The app does not require an account and we do not collect or store personal data on our servers.

1. Data stored on your device

The app saves your best scores and game settings locally on your device only. This data is never sent to us and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and device and app information. We do not receive this data. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Learn more: Google Advertising policies and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Permissions

The app uses only: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (to load ads) and the advertising ID permission (AD_ID, used by the ads SDK). It does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
